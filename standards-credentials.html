Learner recognition

Certificates of completion for each defined pathway.

Certificates recognize successful completion of required learning experiences and portfolio/capstone evidence. They are program credentials issued by Drone Career Explorer / Maison Glamour et Grace and are not FAA airman certificates or operating authority.

Grades 6–8

Drone Career Explorer Foundations Certificate

Earned after completing the six-unit middle-school pathway, required portfolio evidence, and the Drone Career Challenge.

  • 6-unit pathway completed
  • Portfolio evidence submitted
  • Career challenge completed
  • Responsible-use reflection completed
Grades 9–12

Applied Drone Systems & Career Readiness Certificate

Earned after completing the nine-unit high-school pathway, professional-style portfolio artifacts, and capstone mission proposal/defense.

  • 9-unit pathway completed
  • Required portfolio artifacts
  • Capstone mission proposal
  • Presentation/defense or equivalent evidence
Professional

Advanced UAS Operations Readiness Certificate of Completion

Earned after completing the professional readiness pathway and capstone evidence covering governance, safety, automation oversight, data, security, reliability, and advanced-operations context.

  • Professional modules completed
  • Readiness evidence documented
  • Advanced-operations capstone
  • Regulatory-context acknowledgement
Important distinction: These are educational/program certificates. The FAA separately issues aviation credentials such as the Part 107 Remote Pilot Certificate.
Educator professional learning

Drone Career Explorer Program-Issued Educator Microcredential.

The educator microcredential recognizes demonstrated readiness to facilitate the curriculum—not simply participation in a workshop. It can be earned through professional learning plus implementation evidence.

01

Learn

Complete the required Drone Career Explorer professional-learning sequence.

02

Plan

Develop a grade-band implementation plan, including learning setting, materials, safety expectations, and learner supports.

03

Demonstrate

Submit evidence such as a lesson adaptation, facilitation artifact, student-work review, or implementation reflection.

04

Earn

Receive the program-issued educator microcredential and digital recognition after evidence review.

Program-issued microcredential

Educator Facilitator

Recognizes the ability to facilitate Drone Career Explorer using responsible technology practices, evidence-based instruction, career-connected learning, and appropriate implementation safeguards.

Credential integrity

Recognition that means something.

Credential requirements are tied to observable evidence so schools and learners can distinguish program completion from simple participation.

Defined requirementsEach certificate identifies the pathway and evidence required.
Verification-readyCertificates can include issue date, unique credential ID, and verification record when the digital system is activated.
Clear scopeProgram credentials never substitute for FAA certificates, licenses, approvals, or operating authority.
