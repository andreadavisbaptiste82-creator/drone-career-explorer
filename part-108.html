Current regulatory context

Advanced drone operations span current rules and emerging BVLOS standards.

Drone Career Explorer separates what is authorized today from what the FAA has proposed for routine, scalable BVLOS operations.

CURRENT OPERATIONS

Package delivery today: Part 135

FAA commercial small-package delivery uses the existing Part 135 air-carrier certification pathway. BVLOS package-delivery operations also require the applicable FAA exemptions, waivers, airspace authorizations, and operating approvals.

PROPOSED FRAMEWORK

Part 108: proposed BVLOS framework

The FAA's proposed BVLOS rule would establish performance-based requirements for routine advanced operations, including package delivery, agriculture, aerial surveying, public-interest missions, training, demonstrations, recreation, and flight testing.

FAA: Proposed BVLOS Rule →
Educational distinction: Part 135 is an existing certification pathway used for UAS package delivery. Part 108 remains proposed and is presented here as an emerging regulatory framework—not current operating authority.
Industry watch • 2026

Advanced-operations integration is continuing to evolve.

FAA BEYOND Phase 2 runs through 2029, and on August 27, 2026 the FAA announced plans to add up to eight additional state, local, tribal, or territorial lead participants. This work informs scalable advanced UAS integration while the BVLOS rulemaking process continues.
